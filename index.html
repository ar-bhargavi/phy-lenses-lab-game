📘 Quick Theory

Convex lens — 6 cases:
• u > 2F → real, inverted, diminished (F < v < 2F)
• u = 2F → real, inverted, same size (v = 2F)
• F < u < 2F → real, inverted, magnified (v > 2F)
• u = F → image at ∞
• u < F → virtual, erect, magnified (same side)

Concave lens — always virtual, erect, diminished.

Sign convention (Cartesian): distances measured from O; against incident light → negative.
